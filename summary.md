# CRM — Lead Scoring & Prioritization

## What Problem Does This Solve?
Sales teams waste time calling leads who are not ready to buy. This tool automatically scores incoming leads so sales reps focus on high-probability deals first.

## How It Works (Simple Terms)
1. **Inputs**: Takes company size, industry type, website visits, whitepaper downloads, and email click rates.
2. **Analysis**: Evaluates patterns from past deals to calculate a **0–100% Win Chance** for every new lead.
3. **Action Tiers**:
   - 🔥 **HOT (≥ 70%)**: High priority — assign sales representative within 1 hour.
   - ⚡ **WARM (40% – 69%)**: Medium priority — enroll in automated nurture email sequence.
   - ❄️ **COLD (< 40%)**: Low priority — keep in general automated marketing campaign.

## 🗣️ How to Explain This in an Interview (30-Second Pitch)
> *"I built a CRM lead scoring module that ranks incoming sales leads based on company profile and online engagement. It automatically tags leads as HOT, WARM, or COLD so our sales reps focus on top prospects immediately, improving conversion rates and shortening sales cycles."*
